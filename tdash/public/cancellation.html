This policy works together with our Refund Policy and Terms of Service.

If an event is cancelled

  • We’ll email everyone who bought tickets, from [SUPPORT_EMAIL], as soon as the cancellation is confirmed.
  • Our staff will refund every eligible order in full within 14 days of the cancellation announcement. You don’t need to do anything.
  • The refund goes to the card you paid with and covers the full amount you paid, including HST and all fees.
  • Tickets for a cancelled event can no longer be used.
  • If a ticket in your order was transferred, the refund still goes to the original purchaser’s payment method. If you received a ticket by transfer, please settle up with the person who sent it to you. These orders may take longer, but we still aim for 14 days.
  • Haven’t seen your refund after 14 days? Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] with your order number.

If an event is postponed or rescheduled

  • Your tickets stay valid for the new date. You don’t need to do anything.
  • If you can’t attend the new date, email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] within 14 days of the announcement with your order number, and our staff will refund your order in full.
  • After those 14 days, the normal Refund Policy applies again.

If the venue changes

We treat a move to a different venue as a postponement. The same 14-day refund window applies.

What doesn’t count

Small changes, such as start time, set times or a support act, aren’t a cancellation or postponement.
