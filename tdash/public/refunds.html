The short version

All ticket sales are final. We refund tickets only if:

  • the event is cancelled;
  • the event is postponed or rescheduled, or moves to a different venue; or
  • we confirm a mistake on our side, such as a duplicate charge or a paid order that never produced tickets.

We also refund wherever the law requires it.

What you get back

  • The full amount you paid. Our prices already include HST and all fees, so nothing is deducted.
  • The refund goes to the card you paid with. Your bank may take a few business days to show it.
  • We refund whole orders only. We can’t refund some tickets in an order and keep the others.

Cancelled or postponed events

See our Cancellation and Postponement Policy. In short, if an event is cancelled, our staff will refund every eligible order in full within 14 days of the announcement, and you don’t need to ask. If it’s postponed, your tickets stay valid. If you can’t make the new date, email us within 14 days of the announcement.

When we can’t refund

  • The ticket has already been scanned at the door.
  • You can’t attend, changed your mind or bought the wrong number of tickets.
  • Costs outside the ticket, such as travel, parking or a hotel.
  • You were refused entry, or removed, for breaking venue or event rules.

How to ask

Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] with your order number (it starts with ORD-), the email you used to buy, and the reason. Please never send card numbers, passwords or pictures of your ticket QR code.

Transferred tickets

Refunds always go to the original purchaser’s payment method (the card used to buy the order), even if a ticket was transferred to someone else. If you received a ticket by transfer, any refund goes to the person who bought it, so please settle up with the person who sent you the ticket. A refund cancels every ticket on the order, including any that were transferred. Orders with transferred tickets may take us a little longer.

Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank, we may cancel the tickets on that order while the dispute is reviewed. Please contact us first. It’s usually faster.
